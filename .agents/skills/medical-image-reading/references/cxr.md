# Chest radiograph protocol

## Quality gate

Confirm projection/view (PA, AP, lateral, portable/unknown), rotation, inspiration,
exposure/penetration, motion, collimation, full lung and costophrenic-angle coverage,
laterality, and prior availability. Do not apply PA-only size reasoning to an AP or
unknown projection. If only one view is present, state that limitation.

## Blind reading order

1. Devices and external artifacts; trace every visible line/tube along its course.
2. Airway, trachea, carina, mediastinal contour, and hila.
3. Cardiac silhouette and pulmonary vasculature, qualified by projection.
4. Right and left upper/middle/lower lung zones, comparing symmetry and priors.
5. Pleura, apices, costophrenic angles, and diaphragms.
6. Bones and visible soft tissues.

Required checklist keys are `projection_quality`, `airway`, `lungs`, `pleura`,
`cardiac_silhouette`, `mediastinum`, `hila`, `diaphragm`, `bones`, `soft_tissue`,
and `lines_tubes`.

Record morphology and distribution before naming an impression. An absent model
label is not a normal observation. Use tight source-image evidence for a visible
abnormality; do not box an entire hemithorax when a focal sign is intended.

Potentially urgent patterns such as a large pneumothorax with tension features,
free subdiaphragmatic gas, marked mediastinal abnormality, or critically positioned
support devices require immediate human review. Preserve uncertainty when the
single image, projection, or quality cannot establish the diagnosis.
